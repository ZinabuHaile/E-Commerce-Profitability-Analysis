# 🛒 BrightCart: E-Commerce Profitability & Marketing Analytics

## 📖 Project Overview
BrightCart is a multi-channel online retailer selling products across eight categories via their website, mobile app, third-party marketplaces, and social commerce platforms. While the company has generated over $1M+ in gross revenue over the past two years, net margins have been steadily shrinking. 

The objective of this project is to diagnose the root causes of margin compression. By combining transactional data, product costs, and marketing spend, this analysis identifies exactly which channels and categories are driving actual profit, evaluates Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), and provides data-backed recommendations for budget optimization.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
*   **Diagnose Profitability:** Identify which product categories and sales channels are truly profitable after accounting for all costs (COGS, shipping, platform fees, discounts).
*   **Analyze Churn/Returns:** Quantify the impact of return rates on top-line revenue and bottom-line margins.
*   **Optimize Marketing Spend:** Evaluate marketing efficiency across platforms to maximize ROAS.
*   **Cost Reduction Strategy:** Formulate a data-driven plan to safely cut the marketing budget by 20% without disproportionately impacting sales.

## 💾 Data Sources
The analysis is built on three core datasets:
1.  **Order-Level Transactions:** Historical sales data including order IDs, dates, sales channels, discounts, shipping costs, and return status.
2.  **Product Catalog:** Master list of items across the 8 categories, including baseline product costs (COGS) and platform-specific fees.
3.  **Marketing Spend:** Monthly advertising expenditures broken down by marketing platform/campaign.

## 🔍 Key Questions Answered
This project tackles the following strategic questions for the executive team:

### 1. Category Margin Analysis
*   What is the average profit margin by product category? 
*   Which categories are the most and least profitable? 
*   What is the primary driver of these differences (product cost, shipping, returns, or heavy discounting)?

### 2. Channel Performance
*   How does profitability differ across the four core sales channels (Website, Mobile App, Marketplace, Social Commerce)? 
*   Which channel yields the best and worst profit-per-order after accounting for platform-specific fees?

### 3. The Cost of Returns
*   What is the return rate segmented by category and channel? 
*   How much total revenue was lost to returns over the 2-year analysis period?

### 4. Marketing ROAS & Optimization
*   Which advertising platforms deliver the highest Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)? 
*   Are there "bleeding" platforms where ad spend consistently fails to generate a positive return?
*   **Actionable Recommendation:** If forced to cut 20% of the marketing budget, exactly which platforms and specific months should see reductions based on historical data?
